The first point of the match is 1.5. Every point after that is 1. If a score is 1.5, minus takes it back to 0.

Add players for each team. Role can be Raider or Jaafi (stopper). Tap “Raiding now” or “Stopping now” to put that name on the scoreboard.

Five columns slide up one by one. For each player, type the first name, surname, team, and stat, then upload a photo. Upload again to replace it. The picture is shrunk and sent with the match.

Ticker style

Light bar is a tall white strip. Night bar is the same layout in black with white type. Both keep the host on the left and the advert on the right.

INFO row

The top line of the ticker. Hide or show it on its own. The message row and the note row stay as they are.

The clock on the right uses the time zone in Settings. Default is London time. You can change it.

Layout

Board position

Bottom positions stay above the ticker. The logo stays on the left, above the scoreboard.

Animated theme

Sheen, Pulse, Sweep, and Slam are different motions. 3D tilts the board and flips the scores. Colours stay whatever you set below or on the Colours tab.

Colour theme

A preset fills the colours below. Then change any colour. It updates the stream straight away.

Quick swatches

Mode

Winner

Normal shows WINNER / ਜੇਤੂ and ELIMINATED / ਬਾਹਰ. Hold shows “Match result on hold”. Final shows the champion, prize, and player of the match.

Total points follow the score. Raids won and stops follow the + Raid and + Stop buttons. Type raid points, jaafi points, failed raids, and the top names here.

Adverts sit on the bottom right of the ticker while the score is up. They hide for lineup, Top 5, and the other full graphics. A PNG owner photo can be transparent so the person pops out of the panel.

Photos are shrunk before they are sent, so the overlay can update quickly. You can also paste an image link.

Both timers stay hidden until you turn them on. Punjab style usually does not need them.
